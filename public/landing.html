The Problems We Solve

Why most AAC apps fail families

💰 Prohibitive Costs

Legacy AAC apps often cost $250–$300 upfront. We believe communication is a right, not a luxury. Kiwi Voice offers 100% free core features and an affordable pro tier.

🧩 Overwhelming Complexity

Many apps are too difficult for parents and teachers to set up. We’ve built a "Core-First" interface that is intuitive for the whole circle of care—including grandparents.

📉 Shifting Motor Plans

Children learn through muscle memory. Unlike other apps that reflow icons when you search, our grid stays stable so the child can build a consistent motor plan.

🏥 Clinical Gaps

Learning to say "No" or "Wait" is just as important as requesting. We’ve integrated clinical protocols like Denial Tolerance directly into the interface.

Our Mission

From the classroom to the living room

As parents of young children and behavior analysts working in a school setting, we’ve spent our careers fascinated by the way language develops and how it shapes behavior as we grow.

Through our work, we realized something important: We all have a hard time coming up with the right words when we need them most. This challenge starts in early childhood, and it’s a problem that every single one of us deals with from time to time.

We saw first-hand the frustration families face when they can't access the tools they need. There needs to be a clear, simple way for parents, teachers, grandparents, and caregivers to support a child as they find their voice. That’s why we built Kiwi Voice.

We didn't just want to build another app; we wanted to build a bridge between behavior science and the natural routines of daily life.
