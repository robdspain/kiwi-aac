Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 3, 2026

Privacy Commitment: Kiwi Voice is built by Behavior School LLC with a "Privacy by Design" philosophy. We prioritize local-first storage and anonymous synchronization to protect the communication privacy of children and families.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information required to provide communication services and synchronize your data across devices.

A. Anonymous Identifiers

We generate a persistent, anonymous Device ID (using your hardware's unique identifier) to allow for Anonymous Cloud Restore. This allows you to recover your boards and media after an app reinstall without needing to create an account or provide an email address.

B. Usage Data & Analytics

We collect anonymous usage data to help parents and therapists track clinical progress. This includes:

  • Button interaction counts (which words are used most).
  • Session duration and frequency.
  • Clinical milestones (FCR attempts, mastery levels).

Note: This data is stored locally by default and only uploaded if Cloud Sync is enabled.

C. Personal Media

Photos you take and voice prompts you record are stored in your device's Secure IndexedDB. If you enable Cloud Sync, these files are encrypted and stored in our secure database (Neon) to allow for cross-device access.

2. Artificial Intelligence (AI Vision)

Kiwi Voice includes a "Just-In-Time" (JIT) Visual Scene feature using on-device AI.

  • Local Processing: All object detection (identifying chairs, cups, etc. in photos) happens 100% on your device using TensorFlow.js.
  • No Image Uploads for AI: We never transmit your photos to a server for AI analysis. The images stay on your device unless you manually enable Cloud Sync for backup purposes.

3. Biometric Security

You may choose to enable FaceID or TouchID to protect "Adult Settings."

  • We use native system APIs to perform this check.
  • We never see or store your biometric data. Your device's operating system handles the authentication and only provides the app with a "Success" or "Failure" result.

4. Third-Party Services

We use a limited number of trusted partners to manage the app's infrastructure:

  • RevenueCat & Stripe: To manage subscriptions and process payments safely. They handle transaction data according to their own strict privacy standards.
  • Neon: Provides our high-security relational database for Cloud Sync.
  • ARASAAC: We fetch standard symbols from the ARASAAC library.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

As an AAC tool, Kiwi Voice is designed for children. We strictly adhere to COPPA and GDPR-K regulations:

  • We do not require or ask for a child's name, age, or contact information.
  • We do not show third-party advertisements.
  • Parents/Guardians have absolute control over data deletion and synchronization.

6. Data Security & Deletion

Your data is encrypted during transmission and at rest when using Cloud Sync. You may delete your data at any time by using the "Reset All Data" function in the Adult Settings. This will wipe all local data and unlink your Device ID from our cloud database.
