Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Kiwi Voice.

Is Kiwi Voice really free?

↓
Yes! Kiwi Voice is completely free to download and use with core communication features. Pro features (advanced analytics, cloud sync, unlimited contexts) are available for $39/year or $149.99 lifetime. You can start communicating immediately without any payment.

Will this work for my child's specific needs?

↓
Kiwi Voice supports children at all communication levels, from first words to complex sentences. It includes switch access for motor disabilities, customizable grid sizes, and guided progression through 6 skill levels. If your child can use a touchscreen or switch, Kiwi Voice can help them communicate.

How is this different from Proloquo2Go or TouchChat?

↓
Kiwi Voice offers modern 3D characters instead of 90s clip-art, built-in skill training tools (FCR, denial, prompting), AI-powered visual scenes, cross-platform support (not just iOS), and biometric security—all at a fraction of the cost. Plus, it works on web, iOS, and Android, not just one platform.

Does it work offline?

↓
Absolutely! Kiwi Voice is offline-first. All communication features, voice recordings, and progress tracking work without internet. Cloud sync is optional and only used when you want to backup or sync across devices.

Can I use my own photos and voice recordings?

↓
Yes! You can upload custom photos for any symbol and record unlimited custom voices (Mom's voice, Dad's voice, siblings, etc.). You can also import Apple Memoji characters for ultra-personalization.

Is my child's data private and secure?

↓
100%. All data stays on your device by default. We don't track usage, sell data, or know what words your child uses. Optional cloud sync uses encrypted anonymous identifiers. You can export all data anytime. Biometric authentication (FaceID/TouchID) protects adult settings.

What if I lose my device?

↓
With Pro features enabled, your data automatically syncs to secure cloud storage. You can restore everything on a new device instantly. You can also export backups manually anytime. Unlike single-device AAC systems, you'll never lose your child's communication setup.

Can therapists and teachers access progress data?

↓
Yes! You can export detailed progress reports showing word usage, independence levels, skill mastery, and session data. Reports are formatted for easy sharing with SLPs, BCBAs, teachers, and therapy teams. You control what data is shared and when.
